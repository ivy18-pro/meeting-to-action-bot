# Meeting-to-Action AI

**Turn conversations into action.**

Meeting-to-Action AI is an intelligent meeting management platform designed to bridge the gap between discussions and execution. Powered by Google Gemini, it analyzes meeting transcripts, understands conversations, extracts important decisions, identifies actionable tasks, and organizes responsibilities and deadlines.

Unlike traditional meeting summarization tools, Meeting-to-Action AI focuses on what happens *after* a meeting. It converts unstructured discussions into structured, trackable outcomes while identifying missing information, unresolved questions, and unclear commitments.

### Key Features

- **AI-Powered Meeting Analysis:** Uses Google Gemini to understand meeting transcripts and generate meaningful summaries.
- **Decision Extraction:** Identifies important decisions and agreements made during discussions.
- **Automated Task Extraction:** Converts meeting conversations into structured action items.
- **Responsibility Identification:** Extracts task owners and deadlines when explicitly mentioned.
- **Missing Information Detection:** Highlights unassigned tasks, missing deadlines, and unresolved questions instead of making assumptions.
- **Task Management:** Review, manage, and track tasks through their completion.
- **Meeting History:** Maintain organized records of meetings and their AI-generated insights.
- **Persistent Data Management:** Save meeting details, analysis results, and tasks for future reference.

### How It Works

1. Create a meeting by entering its title, date, participants, and agenda.
2. Add the meeting transcript.
3. Submit the transcript for AI analysis.
4. Gemini processes the conversation and extracts summaries, decisions, tasks, owners, and deadlines.
5. Review the AI-generated results and clarify missing information.
6. Confirm extracted tasks and track their progress through the application.

### Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini API
- **Backend:** Server-side API integration
- **Database:** Persistent application storage
- **Development Environment:** Google AI Studio / AI-assisted development

### Vision

Our goal is to make meetings more productive by ensuring that important discussions do not end as forgotten notes. Meeting-to-Action AI transforms conversations into clear responsibilities, measurable progress, and meaningful outcomes.
